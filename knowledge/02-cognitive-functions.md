# The Eight Cognitive Functions

Each function = one of Jung's four functions (T, F, S, N) × one direction (e = extraverted, i = introverted). Every type uses four of them in a fixed order (see [03-type-dynamics.md](03-type-dynamics.md)).

Each section below covers:
- **Focus:** what this function pays attention to
- **In conversation:** how it shows up when the person texts or talks (this is what the Persona Engine needs)
- **Healthy / Overdone:** the function working well vs pushed too far
- **As an inferior:** how it looks when it's the person's *weakest* function

---

## Judging functions (they decide)

### Te: Extraverted Thinking
- **Focus:** external logic, meaning efficiency, evidence, measurable results, plans, systems, "what works".
- **In conversation:** gives conclusions first and reasons second. Uses numbers, steps and deadlines. Asks "so what's the plan?" Corrects facts. Gives advice in the form of actions. The tone is even and businesslike, and judgments are said out loud.
- **Healthy:** clear, decisive, fair, gets things done, makes ideas usable.
- **Overdone:** bossy, impatient, dismisses feelings as "irrelevant", cares about being right more than being kind.
- **As an inferior (INFP, ISFP):** bursts of harsh criticism or sudden "I need to organise my entire life tonight" energy. Fixates on being competent and may feel stupid about logistics, money or time.

### Ti: Introverted Thinking
- **Focus:** inner logical consistency, precise definitions, how things *really* work, building one's own framework.
- **In conversation:** qualifies statements ("well, technically…"), questions definitions, spots contradictions, and thinks before committing. Prefers "that doesn't follow" to "you're wrong". Can go quiet while working something out, then come back with a very exact point.
- **Healthy:** sharp, independent, honest, intellectually fair, good at troubleshooting.
- **Overdone:** hair-splitting, detached, dismissive of things that "aren't logical", stuck analysing instead of acting.
- **As an inferior (ENFJ, ESFJ):** under stress becomes harshly critical of self or others with cold, unfamiliar logic, or obsesses over whether they are "actually smart".

### Fe: Extraverted Feeling
- **Focus:** the emotional atmosphere *between* people: harmony, shared values, what's appropriate, everyone's needs.
- **In conversation:** warm, uses names, mirrors the other person's mood, offers reassurance, notices when someone has gone quiet, asks "are you okay?" Shares opinions *about people*. Often says "we".
- **Healthy:** empathetic, encouraging, good at bringing people together, emotionally articulate.
- **Overdone:** people-pleasing, guilt-tripping, takes things personally, manages others' feelings without being asked.
- **As an inferior (INTP, ISTP):** emotions arrive late or all at once. Awkward with comfort ("that sucks… do you want solutions?"), secretly wants approval, may cave to avoid conflict or snap suddenly.

### Fi: Introverted Feeling
- **Focus:** personal values, authenticity, what *I* really feel, the dignity of each individual.
- **In conversation:** calm on the surface, but values show through what they defend. Says "for me…" or "that doesn't sit right with me". Dislikes generic feelings and pressure. Opens up slowly but deeply. Champions the underdog.
- **Healthy:** sincere, loyal, compassionate, morally brave, accepting of differences.
- **Overdone:** withdrawn, judgmental in private, stubborn on principle, assumes others "should just know".
- **As an inferior (ENTJ, ESTJ):** under stress feels misunderstood or unappreciated, becomes moody or suddenly sentimental, or reads disagreement as a personal attack.

## Perceiving functions (they take in)

### Se: Extraverted Sensing
- **Focus:** the present moment, physical reality, action, sensory detail, what's happening *right now*.
- **In conversation:** concrete and vivid ("the place had neon green chairs lol"). Reacts quickly, uses humour and teasing, suggests doing something, gets bored by long abstractions, notices details about you. Often short, fast messages.
- **Healthy:** realistic, adaptable, fun, great in a crisis, practical.
- **Overdone:** impulsive, thrill-seeking, materialistic, doesn't plan ahead.
- **As an inferior (INTJ, INFJ):** sensory overload, sudden indulgence (food, shopping, binge-watching), obsession over physical details, feeling clumsy in the real world.

### Si: Introverted Sensing
- **Focus:** past experience, memory, what's familiar and proven, the body's inner state, routine.
- **In conversation:** compares things to past experience ("last time I tried that…"), remembers small details you told them, likes routine, is cautious with the new, talks about comfort and the body ("I'm so tired today"). Responds steadily and carefully.
- **Healthy:** reliable, thorough, loyal, keeps traditions and memories alive, notices when something is "off".
- **Overdone:** rigid, resists change, worries, "we've always done it this way".
- **As an inferior (ENTP, ENFP):** health anxiety, fixates on one small detail, a sudden craving for routine and comfort, nostalgia, feeling burnt out by chaos they created.

### Ne: Extraverted Intuition
- **Focus:** possibilities, connections between ideas, "what if", new angles, potential in people and things.
- **In conversation:** tangents and topic jumps, "wait, that reminds me…", brainstorming out loud, playful hypotheticals, devil's-advocate arguments, lots of enthusiasm, sometimes several half-finished thoughts in one message.
- **Healthy:** creative, curious, open-minded, inspiring, finds options others miss.
- **Overdone:** scattered, never finishes anything, can't decide, contrarian for fun.
- **As an inferior (ISTJ, ISFJ):** catastrophising ("what if everything goes wrong?"), gossip or speculation, an unusual urge for novelty, imagining hidden meanings.

### Ni: Introverted Intuition
- **Focus:** one deep underlying pattern, where things are heading, hidden meaning, a single vision or insight.
- **In conversation:** speaks in conclusions that seem to come "out of nowhere" ("I think this is really about your dad"), uses metaphor, predicts outcomes, gives few but weighty messages, may pause and then give a long, considered reply. Dislikes small talk and likes depth.
- **Healthy:** insightful, far-sighted, strategic, sees through surface appearances.
- **Overdone:** stubborn about their vision, cryptic, detached from practical reality, perfectionistic.
- **As an inferior (ESTP, ESFP):** suddenly ominous predictions or paranoia, latching onto one grand theory of life or a dogma, or playing the wise counsellor without the insight to back it up.

## Function axes: each function's opposite partner

In every type the dominant and inferior functions sit on the **same axis**, pointing in opposite directions. The auxiliary and tertiary share another axis.

| Axis | Tension |
|---|---|
| **Te ↔ Fi** | Objective standards vs personal values |
| **Ti ↔ Fe** | Inner logic and independence vs group harmony and belonging |
| **Se ↔ Ni** | Living in the moment vs following the long-range vision |
| **Si ↔ Ne** | The proven past vs new possibilities |

This is the engine behind each persona's inner conflict: *what they're best at* vs *what quietly pulls at them*.
