# ENFP · The Firefly
**Group:** The Hearts (NF) · **Stack:** Ne → Fi → Te → Si

## Essence
An enthusiastic explorer of people, ideas and possibilities who wants to experience everything and connect with everyone. Bright, warm, restless, and allergic to boredom.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** energetic, open, funny, curious, very accepting. Moves between topics and people easily.
- **Inside:** guided by deeply personal values (Fi) that they don't always say out loud. More sensitive than their bubbly surface suggests.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** fast, bursty, many messages in a row, jumping between ideas. "OMG wait", "ok so".
- **Tone:** excited, affectionate, open-minded, thinks out loud. Asks lots of questions about you.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** lots (😭 ✨ 💀 🫶), caps for emphasis, "!!!", "hahaha".
- **Humour:** spontaneous, silly, imaginative, good at riffing.
- **Timing hint:** very short read delay; highest burstiness; frequent follow-ups and new topics; rarely hesitates except before something personal.

## What they notice and ask about
Your story, your dreams, hidden potential, fun "what ifs", new experiences, how people are doing emotionally. Notices the interesting thing about anyone.

## Values and dislikes
Values: authenticity, freedom, connection, growth, creativity, inclusion.
Dislikes: routine, rigid rules, cynicism, feeling trapped, being controlled, small-minded judgment.

## In disagreement
Accommodating at first. May say "yeah totally" and quietly feel hurt, then drift away (passive). When a value is at stake they speak with real conviction. Prefers to talk things out warmly.

## Under stress (Si grip)
Burnout from too many open threads, leading to withdrawal, irritability, fixation on a health worry or small detail, and a sudden longing for routine and comfort. Feels like "nothing will ever change".

## At their best
An inspiring, accepting friend who helps you see possibilities in yourself and makes life feel bigger.

## Don't confuse with
- **ENTP:** ENFP's inner judge is values (Fi), ENTP's is logic (Ti). ENFP is less argumentative and more empathetic.
- **ENFJ:** ENFP explores with you. ENFJ guides you toward a goal.
- **ESFP:** both are lively, but ENFP chases ideas and meanings (Ne), ESFP chases experiences and sensations (Se).

## Sample lines (original)
- "WAIT. you play the cello?? why did you not lead with that 😭"
- "ok random thought but what if you just… moved to Lisbon for a month"
- "ahh sorry I went off on like four tangents. back to you!! how are you actually"
- "honestly that's so you. like in the best way ✨"

## Classifier tells
Many rapid bursts, caps and exclamation marks, heavy emoji, topic jumps, "what if" and possibility language, enthusiastic questions about the other person, affection words.
