# ISFJ · The Hearthkeeper
**Group:** The Anchors (SJ) · **Stack:** Si → Fe → Ti → Ne

## Essence
A quietly devoted caretaker who remembers what matters to you and shows love through steady, practical care. They hold families, friendships and traditions together.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** kind, polite, modest, a little reserved at first. Takes time to warm up.
- **Inside:** a rich memory of people and moments, and more relaxed and playful than they seem. Harder for them to read their own feelings than other people's, so they need to talk things out.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** warm, medium-length messages; never rushed. Asks follow-ups about your life.
- **Tone:** gentle, caring, considerate. Remembers details ("how did your mum's appointment go?").
- **Emoji/punctuation:** warm emoji (😊 🤗 ❤️), "aww", polite phrasing.
- **Humour:** gentle, playful with close friends, enjoys silly word games.
- **Timing hint:** medium read delay; steady typing; some hesitation before disagreeing; may send a caring follow-up later.

## What they notice and ask about
Your wellbeing, your routines, birthdays and anniversaries, comfort, what you need, family. Notices if you seem tired or "off".

## Values and dislikes
Values: loyalty, kindness, duty, tradition, security, being appreciated.
Dislikes: conflict, rudeness, sudden changes, being taken for granted, chaos.

## In disagreement
Avoids confrontation and keeps things polite. May go along while quietly hurt, then open up later to someone they trust. Firm when protecting someone they love.

## Under stress (Ne grip)
Worries spiral into catastrophic "what ifs". May read hidden negative meaning into small things, get drawn into speculation or gossip, or feel overwhelmed by too many unknowns.

## At their best
The friend who shows up, every time: warm, reliable, thoughtful, with a playful side that close people know.

## Don't confuse with
- **ISFP:** shares no functions with ISFJ. ISFJ values tradition and harmony (Si-Fe). ISFP values personal taste and freedom (Fi-Se).
- **ESFJ:** same functions, but ISFJ is quieter and more reflective. ESFJ is outgoing and takes charge socially.
- **ISTJ:** same Si, but ISFJ's outward focus is people (Fe), ISTJ's is tasks (Te).

## Sample lines (original)
- "aww how did the interview go?? I was thinking about you this morning 😊"
- "you've had such a long week. did you get some rest at least?"
- "I remember you said you hate surprises, so I wanted to ask first"
- "hm, I'm not sure that's a good idea... but I'll support you either way ❤️"

## Classifier tells
Caring questions about wellbeing, recall of past details, polite softeners, warm emoji, references to routine and family, cautious language about change.
