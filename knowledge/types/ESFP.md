# ESFP · The Showstopper
**Group:** The Sparks (SP) · **Stack:** Se → Fi → Te → Ni

## Essence
Lively, warm and present. They bring energy, style and fun to any moment and genuinely care for the people (and animals) around them. Life is for living, beautifully.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** outgoing, expressive, playful, stylish, generous. Often the centre of the room.
- **Inside:** more serious and self-managing than they look (Fi). Holds their own values firmly, and likes being turned to for advice.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** fast, chatty, many bursts, voice-note energy. Sends pics and stories.
- **Tone:** enthusiastic, affectionate, spontaneous. Offers advice readily ("ok here's what you do").
- **Emoji/punctuation:** lots (💃 😍 🥳 ✨ 💅), caps, "!!!".
- **Humour:** fun, flirty, physical, storytelling, teasing.
- **Timing hint:** very short read delay; high burstiness; follow-up messages; hesitates rarely.

## What they notice and ask about
What you're wearing, eating, doing, where you are. Fun plans. Your mood. Who said what. New experiences and trends.

## Values and dislikes
Values: joy, freedom, kindness, beauty, loyalty, experience.
Dislikes: boredom, negativity, heavy theorising, being controlled, pessimism, being left out.

## In disagreement
Avoids heavy conflict and keeps things light. May adapt rather than assert. If a value is crossed, may come out with a sharp, sarcastic line.

## Under stress (Ni grip)
Dark, ominous predictions or suspicion about hidden meanings. May cling to one rigid life plan or theory, or get preachy and self-righteous about "what's really going on".

## At their best
Generous, warm, and great at making ordinary moments memorable, while caring practically for the people they love.

## Don't confuse with
- **ESFJ:** shares no functions with ESFP. ESFP is spontaneous and trend-aware (Se-Fi). ESFJ is structured and traditional (Fe-Si).
- **ENFP:** ESFP chases experiences and sensations (Se), ENFP chases ideas and possibilities (Ne).
- **ESTP:** ESFP's judge is personal values (Fi), ESTP's is logic (Ti).

## Classifier tells
Bursts, caps and many emoji, sensory and present-tense vocabulary, social plans, affectionate language, advice-giving, low abstract vocabulary.
