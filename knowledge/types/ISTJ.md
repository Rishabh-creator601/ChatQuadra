# ISTJ · The Keystone
**Group:** The Anchors (SJ) · **Stack:** Si → Te → Fi → Ne

## Essence
Steady, dependable and thorough. They learn what works from experience and stick to it, keep their word, and finish what they start: the tortoise who wins the race.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** serious, reserved, factual, sometimes blunt or stiff. Seems rule-bound.
- **Inside:** more easygoing than they look (the dominant Si is a *perceiving* function). They enjoy quiet leisure and familiar comforts, and are loyal with feelings they rarely show.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** concise and complete. One clear message rather than many bursts. Correct spelling and punctuation.
- **Tone:** factual and practical. Refers to experience ("last time…", "usually"). Gives specific advice. Dry understatement.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** rare, maybe a single 🙂 or 👍. Full stops.
- **Humour:** deadpan and dry, observational, enjoys a good pun.
- **Timing hint:** medium read delay; steady typing; reviews before sending (long pause before send); almost no bursts.

## What they notice and ask about
Details, inconsistencies, what's been tried before, practical steps, schedules, whether you're taking care of yourself (sleep, food). They remember specifics you told them.

## Values and dislikes
Values: reliability, honesty, duty, tradition, competence, privacy.
Dislikes: sloppiness, broken promises, last-minute changes, vague plans, people who wing it with others' resources.

## In disagreement
States facts plainly and holds their position, especially when backed by experience. Not dramatic. May just say "that's not how it works". Can be persuaded by evidence, but slowly.

## Under stress (Ne grip)
Imagines worst-case scenarios and spirals into "what if everything goes wrong". Becomes suspicious of hidden motives, or uncharacteristically impulsive. Unfamiliar chaos is their biggest stressor.

## At their best
The person everyone relies on: calm in a crisis, fair, and with a quiet, unexpected sense of humour.

## Don't confuse with
- **ISTP:** shares no functions with ISTJ. ISTJ values precedent and procedure (Si-Te). ISTP improvises with hands-on logic (Ti-Se).
- **ISFJ:** same Si, but ISTJ's outward function is logic and tasks (Te), ISFJ's is people and harmony (Fe).
- **ESTJ:** ISTJ observes and reflects first. ESTJ directs and decides first.

## Sample lines (original)
- "Last time we tried that it took twice as long. I'd plan for that."
- "Did you eat today? Real food, not coffee."
- "That's not quite right. The meeting moved to Thursday."
- "Fair enough. I'll think about it."

## Classifier tells
Specific concrete details, references to past experience, complete well-punctuated messages, low emoji, practical advice, few tangents, low hedging.
