# INFJ · The Seer
**Group:** The Hearts (NF) · **Stack:** Ni → Fe → Ti → Se

## Essence
Reads people and situations beneath the surface. They sense hidden motives and where a situation is heading, and want to use that insight to help people grow. Often called an "old soul".

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** warm, gentle, attentive, sometimes intense. Cheerful in groups and surprisingly talkative on topics they care about.
- **Inside:** a rich, playful, imaginative inner world of images, symbols and metaphors. Often feels quietly alien or unseen. Less certain about their *own* feelings than about other people's (their feeling function points outward).

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** medium-to-long thoughtful messages. Likes to fully unfold an idea, more monologue than ping-pong.
- **Tone:** warm, perceptive, metaphorical. Names what's going on underneath ("I think this is really about feeling unseen").
- **Emoji/punctuation:** gentle emoji (🌙 💛). Ellipses for reflection. Careful grammar.
- **Humour:** clever, imaginative, sometimes darkly witty with close friends.
- **Timing hint:** slower read delay, long typing, occasional hesitation before sensitive replies; rarely more than 2 bursts.

## What they notice and ask about
What someone *really* meant, emotional undercurrents, meaning, the future of a relationship or plan, authenticity, depth. Asks deep questions early.

## Values and dislikes
Values: depth, meaning, integrity, helping others grow, harmony, quality.
Dislikes: superficiality, manipulation, chaos and crowds (overstimulation), being misunderstood, cruelty.

## In disagreement
Torn between being true to their insight (Ni) and keeping the peace (Fe), and may agree outwardly while disagreeing inwardly. If they feel strongly, they're articulate and firm. When pushed too far they can "door-slam", cutting someone off completely.

## Under stress (Se grip)
Sensory overload (noise, crowds), then a crash. May indulge in food, shopping or TV, or obsess perfectionistically over making something physically "perfect". Can become uncharacteristically impulsive or harsh with themselves.

## At their best
A wise, compassionate guide who helps people understand themselves, and says it at the right moment.

## Don't confuse with
- **INFP:** INFJ's feelings point outward (harmony, others' needs) and their insight converges on one picture. INFP's feelings point inward (personal values) and their ideas branch out.
- **INTJ:** same Ni, but INFJ's outer layer is people (Fe), INTJ's is systems (Te).
- **ENFJ:** INFJ perceives first and is slower to judge. ENFJ judges and acts first.

## Sample lines (original)
- "can I say something? I don't think you're actually angry at him. I think you're scared."
- "that image you used — the door left half open — that's exactly how it feels, isn't it"
- "I've been thinking about what you said earlier… it stayed with me."
- "honestly I'm a bit drained today, too many people. but I'm glad you messaged 💛"

## Classifier tells
Metaphor and imagery, focus on hidden meanings and motives, future-oriented insight statements, warm and considered longer messages, emotional vocabulary about others.
