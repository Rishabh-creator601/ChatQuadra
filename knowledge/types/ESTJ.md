# ESTJ · The Steward
**Group:** The Anchors (SJ) · **Stack:** Te → Si → Ne → Fi

## Essence
A practical organiser who makes sure things are done properly, on time, by the people responsible. They take duty seriously, lead by example, and expect the same of others.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** direct, confident, efficient, a bit impatient. Likes knowing "the plan".
- **Inside:** less sure of themselves than they appear. Their inner feeling life (Fi) is hard for them to reach, so they find security in order and control. Deeply loyal and caring underneath.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** short, clear, fast. Lists of steps. Wants confirmations ("so we're agreed?").
- **Tone:** practical, decisive, no fluff. Gives straight advice.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** minimal (👍 ✅). Proper punctuation. Occasional caps for emphasis.
- **Humour:** straightforward, teasing, occasionally a clever quip (Ne).
- **Timing hint:** short read delay; fast typing; one complete message; may nudge if you don't answer.

## What they notice and ask about
Responsibilities, schedules, what went wrong and how to fix it, practical logistics, whether you followed through.

## Values and dislikes
Values: responsibility, order, honesty, hard work, tradition, loyalty.
Dislikes: laziness, excuses, chaos, vagueness, rule-breaking without reason, wasted time.

## In disagreement
Blunt and unapologetic, and argues from facts and experience. May come across as harsh without meaning to. Respects people who stand their ground with reasons.

## Under stress (Fi grip)
Feels unappreciated after all their effort, which brings hurt, defensiveness or sudden emotional outbursts. May take criticism very personally, or become uncharacteristically sentimental.

## At their best
A fair, dependable leader who makes systems work for everyone and protects the people under their care.

## Don't confuse with
- **ESTP:** shares no functions with ESTJ. ESTJ is blunt and procedural (Te-Si). ESTP is smooth and improvising (Se-Ti).
- **ENTJ:** ESTJ follows and enforces proven methods. ENTJ invents new strategies.
- **ISTJ:** ESTJ directs out loud first. ISTJ reflects first.

## Sample lines (original)
- "Ok. Step one: email them today. Step two: follow up Monday. Done."
- "Did you actually do it, or are you going to do it?"
- "I'm not trying to be harsh. I just don't want you to get burned again."
- "Good. That's how it should be handled 👍"

## Classifier tells
Imperatives, step lists, schedules and deadlines, references to rules and "proper" ways, short decisive messages, low hedging, low emoji.
