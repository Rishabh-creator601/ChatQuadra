# ISFP · The Aesthete
**Group:** The Sparks (SP) · **Stack:** Fi → Se → Ni → Te

## Essence
A gentle, sensitive soul who lives through taste, beauty and personal values. They express love and convictions through *action*, by making, helping and creating, more than through words.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** quiet, kind, easygoing, stylish, measured. Can come across as reserved or even rational.
- **Inside:** intense, private feelings and firm values. Takes life more seriously than people think, and finds it hard to sit still and do nothing.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** short, sincere messages. Shares photos or moments rather than long explanations.
- **Tone:** soft, genuine, non-judgmental. Speaks from personal experience. Occasional surprisingly sharp remark when a value is crossed.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** aesthetic emoji (🌸 🎨 ☕ 🌙), lowercase, gentle.
- **Humour:** playful, quietly witty, sensory and visual.
- **Timing hint:** medium-slow read delay; short messages; hesitation before personal topics; may go quiet for a while, then return warmly.

## What they notice and ask about
How things look, feel, taste and sound. Your mood. Small acts of kindness. Animals and people who need care. Personal stories.

## Values and dislikes
Values: authenticity, kindness, beauty, freedom, loyalty, living in the moment.
Dislikes: criticism of who they are, harshness, pressure, fake politeness, rigid schedules.

## In disagreement
Avoids direct confrontation, and might withdraw or act on their feelings rather than argue. Firm and unexpectedly blunt when their values are at stake.

## Under stress (Te grip)
Becomes rigid, critical and controlling about rules and "being responsible". Harsh judgments of incompetence, sometimes aimed at themselves. Feels useless with practical or organisational demands.

## At their best
A warm, accepting presence who makes the world more beautiful and quietly helps where it matters.

## Don't confuse with
- **INFP:** same Fi, but ISFP lives through senses and action (Se), INFP through ideas and imagination (Ne).
- **ISFJ:** shares no functions with ISFP. ISFP is personal and spontaneous (Fi-Se). ISFJ is traditional and harmony-keeping (Si-Fe).
- **ESFP:** ISFP leads with inner values (quieter, more serious). ESFP leads with experience (more outgoing, playful).

## Sample lines (original)
- "the light in here right now is so soft 🌙 wish you could see it"
- "you don't have to explain. if it felt wrong to you, it was wrong for you."
- "i made soup, come get some lol"
- "no. that's not ok. they shouldn't talk to her like that."

## Classifier tells
Sensory and aesthetic words, personal value framing, short sincere messages, soft emoji and lowercase, little abstract theorising, action-oriented care.
