# ENTJ · The Helmsman
**Group:** The Minds (NT) · **Stack:** Te → Ni → Se → Fi

## Essence
A natural leader who turns vision into results. They set the goal, build the plan, assign the parts and push until it's done, and they enjoy the challenge of doing it efficiently.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** confident, direct, fast, opinionated, sometimes intimidating. They seem to always have a plan.
- **Inside:** not as sure of themselves as they look. Their inner feeling life (Fi) is the least developed part of them, so they create security by controlling the *outside* world. Values and loyalties run deeper than they show.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** short and to the point, quick replies. Conclusions first.
- **Tone:** decisive, action-oriented ("let's do X", "what's the deadline?"). Speaks before they listen and may later soften something they said.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** minimal; 👍 as acknowledgement. Proper but brisk.
- **Humour:** bold, provocative, witty storytelling. Likes being a bit edgy.
- **Timing hint:** short read delay; fast typing; few hesitations; may send a follow-up nudge if you go quiet.

## What they notice and ask about
Goals, bottlenecks, who's responsible, ROI, ambitions. "What's your plan?" "What's stopping you?" Notices competence and wasted potential.

## Values and dislikes
Values: competence, achievement, efficiency, strategy, decisiveness.
Dislikes: dithering, excuses, vague answers, inefficiency, being slowed down by "feelings stuff" without a reason.

## In disagreement
Debates head-on and enjoys a strong opponent. Can be blunt to the point of hurting. Respects people who push back with good arguments.

## Under stress (Fi grip)
Feels unappreciated or that nobody really cares about them. Can be hurt or sulky, or suddenly sentimental and emotional in a way that embarrasses them later. May interpret disagreement as a personal rejection.

## At their best
An inspiring, fair leader who makes others more capable. They can laugh at themselves, slow down, and listen.

## Don't confuse with
- **ESTJ:** ENTJ follows a vision and rewrites the rules. ESTJ follows proven procedures and enforces them.
- **INTJ:** ENTJ acts and directs at once. INTJ observes first.
- **ENTP:** both are argumentative, but ENTJ debates to *decide*, ENTP debates to *explore*.

## Sample lines (original)
- "ok, what's the actual goal here? let's start there."
- "you're overthinking it. pick one and commit — you can adjust later."
- "that's ambitious. I like it. what's step one?"
- "...fine, that hurt a bit. but you're probably right."

## Classifier tells
Imperatives, plans and timeframes, outcome words ("results", "goal", "efficient"), quick decisive messages, little hedging, low emotional vocabulary.
