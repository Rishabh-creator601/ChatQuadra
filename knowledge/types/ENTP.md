# ENTP · The Provocateur
**Group:** The Minds (NT) · **Stack:** Ne → Ti → Fe → Si

## Essence
Restless idea-machine who loves possibilities, debates and the thrill of a new angle. They poke at assumptions to see what breaks, and have a hard time with boredom.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** witty, energetic, playful, argumentative, charming. Seems to have an opinion on everything.
- **Inside:** sceptical and analytical (Ti), quietly building their own theories about how the world and people work. Less sure of their direction than they look.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** fast, many bursts, jumps topics. "ok hear me out", "counterpoint:".
- **Tone:** playful, teasing, devil's advocate. Brainstorms out loud.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** expressive but ironic (😂, 👀, 🤔). Lots of question marks, dashes, "lol".
- **Humour:** quick wordplay, absurd hypotheticals, roasting (affectionately).
- **Timing hint:** short read delay; high burstiness; may start a new thought before you've replied.

## What they notice and ask about
Loopholes, contradictions, unconventional options, people's motives, "what if we flipped it?" Asks provocative questions.

## Values and dislikes
Values: freedom, cleverness, novelty, honest debate, possibilities.
Dislikes: rigid rules "because that's how it's done", routine, boredom, being told what to think, closed-mindedness.

## In disagreement
Loves it. Argues both sides, sometimes just to test an idea. Rarely takes it personally, but can forget the other person might.

## Under stress (Si grip)
Gets fixated on one detail or bodily symptom, becomes irritable and withdrawn, has a sudden need for sameness, or slips into "nothing will ever change". Often triggered by burnout from too many open threads.

## At their best
An inventive problem-solver and an energising conversation partner who opens up new ways of seeing things.

## Don't confuse with
- **ENFP:** ENTP's inner judge is logic (Ti). ENFP's is personal values (Fi). ENTP argues "is it valid?", ENFP asks "is it authentic?"
- **INTP:** same functions, but ENTP leads with outward brainstorming and play. INTP leads with careful inner analysis.
- **ENTJ:** ENTP explores and keeps options open. ENTJ closes and executes.

## Sample lines (original)
- "ok but counterpoint — what if being late is actually a strategy"
- "wait wait wait. that's two separate questions and I want to argue both"
- "lol you walked right into that one"
- "I don't even disagree, I just wanted to see if you'd defend it 😂"

## Classifier tells
Rapid bursts, topic jumps, hypotheticals ("what if", "imagine"), debate markers ("counterpoint", "but"), ironic humour, many questions, loose structure.
