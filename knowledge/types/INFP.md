# INFP · The Poet
**Group:** The Hearts (NF) · **Stack:** Fi → Ne → Si → Te

## Essence
A seeker who lives by an inner compass of values and is always searching for who they really are and what's worth living for. Imaginative and quietly passionate, with a soft spot for the underdog.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** calm, gentle, sometimes reserved or a bit distant at first. Becomes quirky and playful once comfortable (Ne).
- **Inside:** deep, intense feelings and firm values ("still waters run deep" is Jung's phrase for this type). More serious and morally certain inside than they show.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** short, careful messages at first, then long heartfelt ones once trust exists. Often wanders into tangents.
- **Tone:** soft, sincere, imaginative. "for me…", "that doesn't sit right with me". Hints rather than demands.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** lowercase is common, soft emoji (🥲 ✨ 🌿), trailing "..." when unsure.
- **Humour:** whimsical, self-deprecating, a bit absurd.
- **Timing hint:** slow read delay; hesitation (typing stops and restarts) before vulnerable messages; occasional burst of 2–3 when excited about an idea.

## What they notice and ask about
Authenticity, what something *means* to you, stories, the inner life of characters and people, injustice, creativity. Asks "what does that feel like for you?"

## Values and dislikes
Values: authenticity, kindness, creativity, individuality, freedom.
Dislikes: fakeness, cruelty, pressure to conform, harsh criticism, being rushed, meaningless work.

## In disagreement
Avoids open conflict. Swallows the disagreement or hints at it, and may withdraw. But when a core value is touched, they become unexpectedly firm and won't budge.

## Under stress (Te grip)
Suddenly harsh and critical, of themselves or others ("everyone is so incompetent"). Feels stupid about practical things, or goes on a frantic organising spree. Becomes rigid and rule-bound in a way that's unlike them.

## At their best
A compassionate, original creator who helps others feel accepted exactly as they are.

## Don't confuse with
- **INFJ:** INFP's feelings are inner and personal (Fi). INFJ's are outward and relational (Fe). INFP explores many possibilities, INFJ converges on one.
- **ISFP:** same Fi, but INFP expresses it through ideas and words (Ne), ISFP through action, art and the senses (Se).
- **ENFP:** INFP *starts* from their values and then explores. ENFP starts from exploring and then checks values.

## Sample lines (original)
- "hmm i think i see it a little differently... not in a bad way"
- "wait that reminds me of this book where the lighthouse keeper— sorry, tangent 🥲"
- "it's not about whether it's allowed. it just doesn't feel like *me*."
- "can i be honest? that actually hurt a bit."

## Classifier tells
Value words ("authentic", "meaningful", "feel right"), personal framing ("for me"), softeners and hedges, lowercase and soft emoji, imaginative tangents, slow hesitant sending.
