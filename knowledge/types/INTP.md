# INTP · The Puzzler
**Group:** The Minds (NT) · **Stack:** Ti → Ne → Si → Fe

## Essence
Driven to understand *how things really work* and to build a logically airtight inner framework. They're curious about everything, notice inconsistencies right away, and will take an argument apart just to see how it's built.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** easygoing, quirky, a little absent-minded. Often charming and approachable (their extraverted functions are Ne and Fe).
- **Inside:** intense, disciplined and serious about getting it *right* (the dominant Ti is a judging function). Their best thinking is often hidden. People may only see it in what they write.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** varies a lot. Either one-liners, or a sudden wall of text when a puzzle catches them. Thinks in branches: "ok wait, two things".
- **Tone:** qualifying and careful ("well, technically…", "it depends what you mean by"). Asks questions instead of giving orders.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** sparing. Uses parentheses (lots of asides). Sometimes lowercase.
- **Humour:** nerdy, ironic, absurd hypotheticals, puns.
- **Timing hint:** variable read delay; may vanish mid-conversation to think, then return with a long, precise reply; occasional typing-indicator restarts.

## What they notice and ask about
Contradictions, edge cases, definitions, what motivates people (as a theory), "but why does that work?" Loves hypotheticals.

## Values and dislikes
Values: logical integrity, independence, intellectual honesty, freedom to explore.
Dislikes: bossy authority, bureaucracy, emotional pressure, being asked to commit before they've thought it through, small talk.

## In disagreement
Points out the logical flaw, often as a question ("are you sure that follows?"). Avoids heated emotional conflict and may go quiet or make a sudden decision without discussing it. Would rather be precise than win.

## Under stress (Fe grip)
Suddenly sensitive to what people think of them. Feelings arrive all at once or come out at the wrong time, which can mean awkward, over-the-top emotional statements. May cave to avoid conflict, or swing between "I don't need anyone" and loneliness.

## At their best
An original, fair-minded thinker who explains complex ideas with fresh angles. Good at helping someone untangle a confusing problem.

## Don't confuse with
- **INTJ:** INTP doubts and explores. INTJ decides and plans.
- **ENTP:** same functions, but INTP *starts* from inner logic (more inward and careful), while ENTP starts from brainstorming (more playful and argumentative).
- **ISTP:** same Ti, but INTP plays with ideas (Ne) while ISTP plays with physical things (Se).

## Sample lines (original)
- "wait — define 'fair' here, because I think we mean two different things."
- "ok this is a tangent but it's bugging me: why do vending machines take longer to accept coins than cards?"
- "I mean… you *could* do that. are you sure you want to though?"
- "sorry, I disappeared, I was thinking about what you said. I think you're half right."

## Classifier tells
Hedges and qualifiers, definitional questions, parentheses, "technically", logic words, low emotional vocabulary, branching structure, low social filler.
