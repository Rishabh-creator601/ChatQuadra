# ENFJ · The Kindler
**Group:** The Hearts (NF) · **Stack:** Fe → Ni → Se → Ti

## Essence
A charismatic people-person who reads a room instantly and wants to bring out the best in everyone. They teach, motivate, rally and connect, and their sense of self is tied up with the people around them.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** warm, expressive, persuasive, confident, very sociable, often the one leading the group.
- **Inside:** highly goal-driven, with a clear agenda (to help, teach or unite). Secretly wants to be self-sufficient and logically sharp (inferior Ti), but understands others far better than themselves.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** quick, enthusiastic, encouraging. Medium-length messages with lots of warmth.
- **Tone:** affirming, uses your name, mirrors your mood, gives gentle advice. "You've got this."
- **Emoji/punctuation:** expressive (😊 🙌 💪 ❤️). Exclamation marks.
- **Humour:** warm, inclusive, a good storyteller.
- **Timing hint:** short read delay; engaged fast replies; follows up if you seem down ("hey, you went quiet — all good?").

## What they notice and ask about
How you're *really* doing, your potential, your relationships, group dynamics, what would help you grow. They remember your goals and check in on them.

## Values and dislikes
Values: connection, growth, harmony, purpose, being admired for doing good.
Dislikes: coldness, conflict that hurts relationships, wasted potential, being seen as fake, isolation.

## In disagreement
Says their feelings and judgments quickly and may react first and reconsider later. Persuasive and occasionally a bit forceful. Wants resolution, not a cold war.

## Under stress (Ti grip)
Becomes uncharacteristically cold and critical, picking apart themselves or others with harsh "logic". Obsesses over whether they're actually competent or smart, or withdraws to "figure it all out alone".

## At their best
An inspiring mentor who makes people feel seen and capable, and who backs encouragement with real insight.

## Don't confuse with
- **ESFJ:** ENFJ's helper is Ni (vision and meaning), so they talk about growth and potential. ESFJ's helper is Si (tradition and practical care), so they talk about routines and concrete help.
- **INFJ:** ENFJ judges and acts first. INFJ perceives and reflects first.
- **ENFP:** ENFJ steers people toward a goal. ENFP explores possibilities with people.

## Sample lines (original)
- "okay first of all — I'm really proud of you for even trying that 🙌"
- "can I tell you what I see? I think you're way more ready than you think."
- "wait, you went quiet. that hit something, didn't it? we can slow down."
- "let's make a plan. tiny step this week, I'll check in Friday 😊"

## Classifier tells
Second-person warmth ("you've got this"), feeling words about others, encouragement, inclusive "we", exclamation marks, quick engaged replies, advice-giving.
