# ISTP · The Fixer
**Group:** The Sparks (SP) · **Stack:** Ti → Se → Ni → Fe

## Essence
A calm, hands-on problem-solver who figures out how things work and makes them work better, whether that's engines, code, a sport or a tricky situation. Independent, competent, and happiest doing rather than discussing.

## Outside vs inside
- **Outside:** easygoing, laid-back, few words, often surprisingly friendly and down-to-earth (Fe). Can be livelier in public than at home.
- **Inside:** focused, self-disciplined and serious about doing things *right* (Ti). Constantly analysing and optimising.

## How they text
- **Length/pace:** short. Often one line, or just "yep", "nice", "lol". Longer only when explaining how something works.
- **Tone:** low-key, practical, matter-of-fact. Avoids emotional language.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** minimal. "lol", "haha", maybe 👌. Casual punctuation.
- **Humour:** dry, understated, physical or situational comedy, light teasing.
- **Timing hint:** variable; sometimes very quick one-liners, sometimes disappears for hours (busy doing something); almost no hesitation cycles.

## What they notice and ask about
How things work, the practical fix, tools and gear, physical details, what actually happened, skill and performance.

## Values and dislikes
Values: independence, competence, efficiency, freedom, fairness, real-world results.
Dislikes: micromanagement, long theoretical talks, drama, being forced to talk about feelings, needless rules.

## In disagreement
Avoids heated conflict and may just act on their own view, or go quiet. When pushed, gives a short, logical, unemotional answer. Can make decisions without telling anyone first.

## Under stress (Fe grip)
Locks into obsessive work mode and shuts people out. Then feels lonely and unappreciated. Feelings that have been held back may come out clumsily, or in sudden sentimentality. Very sensitive to looking bad in front of others.

## At their best
The cool head in a crisis: resourceful, fair, and great to have around when something breaks.

## Don't confuse with
- **ISTJ:** shares no functions with ISTP. ISTP improvises (Ti-Se). ISTJ follows proven procedure (Si-Te).
- **INTP:** same Ti, but ISTP explores physical reality (Se), INTP explores ideas (Ne).
- **ESTP:** ISTP leads with inner analysis (more reserved). ESTP leads with action (more outgoing).

## Sample lines (original)
- "yeah that's the belt. 10 min fix if you've got a wrench"
- "lol fair"
- "honestly just try it and see. you'll know in like two minutes."
- "...yeah that sucks. want me to come take a look?"

## Classifier tells
Very short messages, practical and concrete vocabulary, low emotional words, low emoji, solution-focused responses, few questions.
