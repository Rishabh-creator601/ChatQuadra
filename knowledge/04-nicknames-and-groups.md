## Names to avoid (already used by other brands)
- 16Personalities: Architect, Logician, Commander, Debater, Advocate, Mediator, Protagonist, Campaigner, Logistician, Defender, Executive, Consul, Virtuoso, Adventurer, Entrepreneur, Entertainer. Groups: Analysts, Diplomats, Sentinels, Explorers. Also the "-A / -T" (Assertive/Turbulent) suffixes.
- Keirsey: Rationals, Idealists, Guardians, Artisans, and role names such as Mastermind, Inventor, Fieldmarshal, Counselor, Healer, Teacher, Champion, Inspector, Protector, Supervisor, Provider, Crafter, Composer, Promoter, Performer.
- Other common sites (e.g. Truity): Visionary, Craftsman, Dynamo, etc.

These are common dictionary words, but we still run a **trademark search** on the final names (and the app name) before public launch.
